Warm-up (5 min)

Discuss with your partner:

1. What is your dream job? Why?
2. How important is it to enjoy your job?
3. Which matters more: a high salary or being useful to society?
4. What influences career choice: interest, money, parents' advice?
💡 Useful language: I'd love to… / I'm keen on… / For me, the priority is… / It depends on…
Elicit ideas, accept all answers. Board: "dream job — работа мечты". Push for full sentences. Link to Module 3 theme: jobs and responsibility to society.

Reading (10 min)

Choosing a Career: My Dream Job — click any highlighted phrase to see the translation inline.

Every teenager faces the moment when they must make a choice that will shape their whole life. Choosing a profession is not just about money — it is about finding a balance between personal interests and the needs of society. Doctors save lives, teachers educate the next generation, engineers build the future. Every profession contributes to the development of the country.

My dream job is to become a software engineer. I have always been passionate about technology and the way it changes the world. To achieve my goal, I need to get a good education and gain practical experience. First, I will take my final exams and enter a university, where I will study programming and mathematics.

Of course, the path to a dream job is not easy. Young people often face tough competition when they apply for their first job. Employers want candidates with experience, but how can you get experience without a job? The answer is to start with internships and volunteering.

In conclusion, I believe that a profession should be chosen with the heart but checked with the head. If you love what you do, you will never treat work as a burden. And if your job is useful to other people, you will feel that your life truly matters.

Grammar: Infinitive vs Gerund (7 min)

📌 Remember: decide to do, want to do, hope to do, manage to do BUT enjoy doing, avoid doing, keep doing, be interested in doing, be good at doing. After prepositions → -ing.

Complete the sentences. Type the correct form (to + verb or verb-ing):

Answers: 1) to apply 2) working 3) to make 4) earning 5) in choosing 6) to gain 7) facing 8) to become. Fast finishers: write 2 sentences about their own career plans using both patterns.

Speaking: Pair Work (7 min)

Ask and answer with your partner:

1. What qualities does a good software engineer / teacher / doctor need?
2. Would you like to work abroad or in your home town? Why?
3. Is it better to love your job or to earn a lot?
4. Who influences your career choice most — you, your parents or society?
5. What jobs will disappear in 20 years? What jobs will appear?
6. Should students start working while still at school? Why (not)?

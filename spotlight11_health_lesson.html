Задачи

❓ Проверь понимание:
  1. How long has James been feeling unwell? (For three days.)
  2. What are his symptoms? (An earache, a high temperature and he feels dizzy.)
  3. What does the nurse advise him to do? (Take a painkiller and see the doctor at 4 pm.)

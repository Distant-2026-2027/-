Шпаргалка: must / mustn't / should

must — обязан: You must keep your distance from wild animals.

mustn't — запрещено: You mustn't feed the alligators.

should / shouldn't — совет: You should wear boots in snake areas.

📚 Чтение: Wild Animals of the USA

The United States is famous for its wildlife. In the south, in the swamps of Florida, lives the American alligator. It can be four metres long! Alligators usually hunt fish, birds and small animals. They wait motionless in the water and attack very fast.

In the deserts and forests there are rattlesnakes. Their venom is very dangerous, but these snakes never attack people first — they only warn with a rattling sound.

In the mountains of the west lives the grizzly bear. It is big and strong, but it prefers berries and fish to people. If you meet a grizzly, stay calm and back away slowly.

The raccoon is smaller and friendlier. This clever animal with a black mask on its face lives near rivers and even in cities.

Many wild animals in the USA are endangered today. National parks protect their habitats, and rangers warn visitors about the rules: never feed wild animals, watch where you step and keep your distance.

Вопросы к тексту

1. Where do alligators live? — In the swamps of Florida.

2. How does a rattlesnake warn people? — With a rattling sound.

3. What does a grizzly bear prefer to eat? — Berries and fish.

4. What does a raccoon have on its face? — A black mask.

5. What do national parks and rangers do? — They protect habitats and warn visitors about the rules.

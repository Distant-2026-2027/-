🔁 Повторение: Present Perfect (ever / never)

Для рассказа о впечатлениях нужен Present Perfect: have/has + V3. Лесенка тем: «путешествие → впечатления → достопримечательности».
Have you ever been to London? — Ты когда-нибудь был в Лондоне?
I have never seen Big Ben. — Я никогда не видел Биг-Бен.
She has visited three countries this year. — Она посетила три страны в этом году.
What an amazing view! — Какой потрясающий вид!
It is the most magnificent building I have ever seen. — Это самое великолепное здание, которое я когда-либо видел.
Сравните: ever — «когда-либо» (вопросы), never — «никогда» (отрицания). После never глагол уже отрицательный по смыслу — not не нужен.

📚 Чтение: The UK and its Landmarks

The United Kingdom is famous for its historic landmarks. London, the capital, attracts millions of visitors every year. Big Ben, the great clock tower, and Tower Bridge over the River Thames are the symbols of the city. The Tower of London is an ancient fortress built in the 11th century. Today it is a museum where you can see the Crown Jewels.
Not far from London there is Windsor Castle — the Queen's favourite residence. In the south of England you can visit Stonehenge, a mysterious monument of huge stones that is more than four thousand years old. In Scotland, on an old volcano, stands Edinburgh Castle with a breathtaking view of the city. All these places are definitely worth visiting!

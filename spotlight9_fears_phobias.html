📚 Чтение: Fears and Phobias

Everyone feels fear sometimes. Fear is a basic emotion that keeps us alive. When we are afraid, our heart beats faster, we get goosebumps and our body prepares to fight or run away. This is called the "fight or flight" reaction.

A phobia is different. It is a strong, irrational fear of something that is not really dangerous. People with phobias know that their fear is irrational, but they can't control it. For example, a person with arachnophobia is scared to death even when they see a small, harmless spider.

Common phobias include: arachnophobia (spiders), claustrophobia (closed spaces), acrophobia (heights) and glossophobia (public speaking). Some people even have cynophobia — the fear of dogs.

Where do phobias come from? Scientists say that many phobias start after a frightening experience. If a child falls from a tree, he may develop a fear of heights.

The good news is that people can overcome their phobias. Doctors say we should face our fears slowly, one at a time. Then, little by little, the fear will melt away.

3. What is glossophobia? — The fear of public speaking.

5. How can people overcome phobias? — By facing fears slowly, one at a time.

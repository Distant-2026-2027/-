Шпаргалка: Past Continuous

Действие шло в определённый момент в прошлом: was / were + глагол с -ing.

ЛицоФормаПример
I / He / She / Itwas watchingI was watching TV at 6 pm yesterday.
We / You / Theywere playingThey were playing football at that time.

Прерванное действие: While mum was cooking, the phone rang. — Пока мама готовила, зазвонил телефон.

Маркеры: while, when, all day, yesterday at 5 o'clock. Вопрос: What were you doing at 8 pm?

🗣 Диалог: делимся новостями

Клик по реплике — перевод всплывёт снизу.

Tom: Guess what, Kate! You'll never believe it!
Kate: What? Tell me!
Tom: Our school football team won the city cup on Saturday!
Kate: No way! Really?
Tom: Yes! And guess how it happened. While we were training last month, our captain broke a record — he ran the fastest 100 metres!
Kate: That's amazing! What about Masha? I heard she was in a TV interview!
Tom: Right! She was speaking about our school project while the reporter was filming.
Kate: How exciting! Did you hear about Oleg and his dog?
Tom: No, what happened?
Kate: His dog Rex rescued a kitten! It was raining, and the kitten was sitting under a tree. Shall I tell you more?
Tom: Wow! Rex is a real hero. Tell me more!

📚 Чтение: A Real Hero

LOCAL NEWS • LAST SATURDAY

Last Saturday was a usual day. Oleg was walking in the park with his dog Rex. While they were walking near the small river, Oleg heard a strange noise. A little kitten was sitting under a tree and crying. It was raining, and the kitten was cold and scared.

Oleg acted quickly. While the rain was falling, he was looking for help. Rex was barking and running around like a real rescuer. Soon a woman from the animal shelter arrived. She was carrying a warm blanket.

A local news reporter arrived too. While the woman was taking care of the kitten, the reporter was interviewing Oleg. «I was just walking my dog,» Oleg said, «but Rex understood everything!»

Now the kitten is safe and warm. The shelter gave it a name — Lucky. The story made the local news, and everybody at school is proud of Oleg and Rex. Good news travels fast!

Вопросы к тексту

1. Where was Oleg walking? — In the park with his dog Rex.

2. What was the kitten doing? — It was sitting under a tree and crying.

3. What was the weather like? — It was raining.

4. Who took care of the kitten? — A woman from the animal shelter.

5. What is the kitten's new name? — Lucky.

🏠 Домашнее задание

Основное: новостная заметка «Breaking news from my weekend» (50–70 слов) для школьного сайта. План: чем ты занимался(лась) в субботу или воскресенье → что случилось (что делали люди в тот момент) → кто и как отреагировал → вывод. Используй Past Continuous (was/were + -ing) с while или when, не менее 2 фраз для обмена новостями (Guess what! That's amazing! Did you hear about…?) и не менее 5 слов урока.

Звёздочка (по желанию): запиши диалог «делимся новостями» с другом или членом семьи (6–8 реплик): *Guess what! — What? Tell me! — You'll never believe it…*

Критерии (9 баллов): структура заметки (2), Past Continuous (2), фразы для обмена новостями (2), лексика урока — не менее 5 слов (2), оформление (1).

Шкала: 8–9 — «5»; 6–7 — «4»; 4–5 — «3».

Примечание: онлайн — текст в чат или фото рукописной работы; очно — в тетради.

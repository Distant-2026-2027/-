📚 Чтение: Careers of the Future

The world of work is changing very fast. New technologies create new professions every year. Many jobs that existed twenty years ago have disappeared, and new ones have appeared.

What are the modern professions of the world? A software developer writes computer programs and creates apps. A data analyst studies large amounts of information and helps companies make smart decisions. A drone operator controls flying machines that deliver packages and take photos from the sky. A robotics engineer designs robots for factories and hospitals. An ecologist studies nature and protects the environment. A genetic engineer works with DNA and helps to cure diseases.

What skills do modern employers want? Creativity, teamwork, foreign languages and the ability to learn all your life. Robots will do simple and boring jobs, but people will do creative and human jobs.

How can young people prepare for the future? Experts say: choose a profession you love, study hard and never stop learning. If you do this, you will have a successful career — even in professions that don't exist yet.

Вопросы к тексту

1. Why does the world of work change fast? — Because new technologies create new professions.

2. What does a data analyst do? — Studies big data and helps companies make smart decisions.

3. What does a drone operator control? — Flying machines that deliver packages and take photos.

4. What skills do modern employers want? — Creativity, teamwork, foreign languages and lifelong learning.

5. What is the main advice for young people? — Choose a profession you love and never stop learning.

🏠 Домашнее задание

Основное: монолог-эссе «My Future Profession» (140–180 слов). План: профессия → почему выбрал(а) → что делаешь уже сейчас (be going to) → планы после школы (will, Present Continuous) → к 2030 году (Future Perfect) → вывод. Используй не менее 5 слов урока и не менее 3 разных форм будущего времени.

Звёздочка (по желанию): факт-файл о современной профессии мира по образцу учебника: name → skills needed → why it is interesting → will this job exist in 20 years? Итог — один абзац на английском.

Критерии (9 баллов): соответствие плану (2), лексика урока — не менее 5 слов (2), будущие времена — не менее 3 форм (2), орфография (2), оформление (1).

Шкала: 8–9 — «5»; 6–7 — «4»; 4–5 — «3».

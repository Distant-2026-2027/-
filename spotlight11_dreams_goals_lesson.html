📚 Чтение: Goals and Dreams

Every person has dreams. Some children dream of becoming astronauts, others imagine themselves as famous singers. But a dream is only the beginning. To make a dream come true, you need a goal and a plan.

Psychologists say that people who write down their goals achieve them more often. A good goal is specific and realistic. Instead of saying "I want to be successful", say "I hope to pass my exams and enter university next year". Big goals feel impossible, but small steps keep you moving forward.

Choosing a profession is one of the most important decisions in life. Some people follow in their parents' footsteps; others prefer to make their own path. Experts advise young people to try different activities: volunteering, school clubs, part-time jobs. You may discover a talent you never knew you had.

Of course, the road to a dream is not easy. You may fail an exam or lose a competition. But failure is not the end — it is experience. People who succeed in life are not those who never fall, but those who never give up.

So keep dreaming, set your goals, and take the first step today. In the words of Eleanor Roosevelt: "The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."

Вопросы к тексту

1. What do people need to make a dream come true? — A goal and a plan.

2. What kind of goal is a good goal? — Specific and realistic.

3. Why should young people try different activities? — To discover a talent.

4. What is failure, according to the text? — Not the end, but experience.

5. Who succeeds in life? — People who never give up.

Max: Hi, Kate! Are you free tonight?
Kate: I think so. Why? What's up?
Max: Would you like to join me? There's a new cartoon on — people say it's exciting!
Kate: Oh, I'd love to! I'm a fan of cartoons!
Max: Great! What time shall we meet? How about 5 o'clock?
Kate: Shall we say 5:30 at the cinema entrance? I finish my sports practice then.
Max: Fine! So, we meet at 5:30. Popcorn?

📚 Чтение: My Friend's Free Time

My best friend is called Maxim. He is twelve years old, and we are in the same class. Maxim is never bored at the weekend because he has got a lot of hobbies.

Every evening he plays computer games with his team. He usually surfs the Net after dinner and finds interesting facts about space. At the weekend he often goes skateboarding in the park with his brother. He always listens to music on the way to school.

Maxim loves reality shows — he thinks they are exciting. He doesn't like boring TV dramas and he never watches the news. He sometimes goes to the cinema with me: last Saturday we watched a new science fiction film and had a whale of a time.

My friend says: «Free time with friends is the best time». I agree with him. What about your friends? What do they do in their free time?

Вопросы к тексту

1. How old is Maxim? — He is twelve.

2. What does he do every evening? — He plays computer games.

3. What does he do at the weekend? — He often goes skateboarding.

4. What TV shows does he like? — Reality shows — they are exciting.

5. What does Maxim say about free time? — «Free time with friends is the best time».

🏠 Домашнее задание

Основное: мини-рассказ «My friend's free time» (60–80 слов). План: кто твой друг и сколько ему/ей лет → чем он/она занимается каждый день → любимые телепередачи и почему → что вы делаете вместе → вывод. Используй Present Simple (глагол с -s после he/she), наречия частотности (always, usually, often, sometimes, never), like + -ing и не менее 5 слов урока.

Звёздочка (по желанию): запиши по образцу диалог-приглашение (4–6 реплик): *Are you free tomorrow? — I'd love to! How about 3 o'clock? — Count me in!*

Критерии (9 баллов): структура рассказа (2), Present Simple в 3-м лице (2), наречия частотности (1), like + -ing (1), лексика урока — не менее 5 слов (2), оформление (1).

Шкала: 8–9 — «5»; 6–7 — «4»; 4–5 — «3».

Примечание: онлайн — текст в чат или фото рукописной работы; очно — в тетради.
